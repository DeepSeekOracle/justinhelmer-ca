Who I am

I am Justin Helmer. Listeners know the records as Excavationpro. Longer creative work also uses Lightfather. I make experimental hip-hop and electronic texture on my own time, without a label telling me what a song is allowed to be. The streams are free to hear. The masters stay mine.

If you came from a store badge, a live room, or a search for the person behind the catalog, you are in the right place. Stay long enough to pick a door: play, read, or walk the rest of the lattice.
