Put “Justin Helmer” and the reason in the subject: press, booking, permission, or site issue. Allow a few business days for a reply.

What to include

  • Press: outlet, angle, deadline, and whether you need an interview or a quote.
  • Permission: track title, intended use, platform, and whether money is involved.
  • Broken link: the page URL and what you expected to find.
  • Privacy: the email you used and what you want changed or removed.

Do not send passwords, API keys, or private audio you do not want a stranger to hear.

Response times and scope

This is a small independent project, not a 24-hour support desk. Most messages get a reply within a few business days. Press and permission requests may take longer because they need a real decision rather than a quick answer. A precise request is faster than a vague one.

Do not send passwords, API keys, unreleased audio you do not want a stranger to hear, or a link that expires in an hour. If you are reporting a broken page, include the URL and what you expected to find.
