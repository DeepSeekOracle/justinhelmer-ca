How to listen to the catalog

The canonical free player lives at asiancoastline.com. This page is the guide: what to open, what you can do, and where the stores and lyrics fit.

Guide · Justin Helmer · Updated 2026-10-20 · 6 min read · listenplayerguide

Open the free player The catalog

Open the canonical player

The full browser player is at asiancoastline.com/listen.html. It carries the public catalog: search by title or ISRC, filter by album or Haven audio, queue tracks, shuffle, run radio, save local favorites, crossfade, set a sleep timer, and install the shell as an app.

This domain does not run a second copy of the same 11,000-track catalog. One player is easier to maintain, faster to fix, and honest about where the listening room actually is. The name portal points to the room; the room does the playing.

No account, no upload

You do not need a signup. Favorites and queue are stored in your browser, not on an account. The player does not upload your files or build a listening profile. If you clear site data, the local favorites clear with it.

What to do first

  1. Open the player.
  2. Search an album or track title rather than scrolling the whole catalog.
  3. Use the queue for three or four tracks; turn crossfade off to hear the transitions as recorded.
  4. Use radio when you want the player to keep going.
  5. Use the heart for a local favorites list and the share button for a link.

Stores and lyrics

If you want the catalog where you already listen, use the official smart links: ffm.to/justin-helmer for the name and ffm.to/eovnvo9 for Excavationpro. The lyric vault holds the words with a mini player for mapped streams. The music page explains the catalog and the master/stream line.

Troubleshooting

  • One track will not start. Try another track; if only one fails, the public file may be missing. The ledger in the player shows what the catalog expected.
  • Playback stops on mobile. Some browsers suspend background audio; keep the tab active or use the installed shell.
  • The list is empty. Clear the search and set the filter back to All.
  • No sound. Check the player volume, the system volume, and whether another tab owns the audio session.

A five-minute session

Open the player, search an album title, and queue the first three tracks. Turn crossfade off so you hear the transitions as recorded. If you are on a phone, add the player to the home screen first; the installed shell opens faster and gives the audio more room.

After three tracks, open the lyric vault and find one song you just heard. The words and the stream are two views of the same work. If a track fails, note whether the other tracks on the album play; that tells you whether it is a single missing file or a network problem.

What the player does not do

It does not upload your files, require an account, or build a listening profile. It does not sell the catalog or unlock a different version with a payment. It is a public listening room, not a storefront with a play button. The stores are linked separately for people who want them.

It also does not guarantee permanent availability. The catalog is maintained; a file can move. The ledger is the reference, and the contact page is the repair route.

FAQ

Why is the player on another domain?

asiancoastline.com is the listening room. justinhelmer.ca is the name portal. One canonical player avoids duplicate catalogs and duplicate maintenance.

Do I need an account?

No. Favorites and queue are local to your browser.

Can I download the tracks?

The player streams; it is not a download store. Store links are the purchase route where available.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The player is responsive and can be installed to the home screen.
